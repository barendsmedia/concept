01 — About

Built on the deck, not behind a desk.

ALKA Project Support was founded in 2023 by Aron Post. His career started in scaffolding in 2013 and grew into rope access and coating — hands-on experience that now shapes how every project is planned, staffed and delivered.

We provide flexible, tailored solutions worldwide with certified professionals who work to international standards. Quality, safety and sustainability are not add-ons: they are ISO-certified and part of every job we take on.
